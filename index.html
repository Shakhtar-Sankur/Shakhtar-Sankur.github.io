About

One company, two products, one thesis.

The people who move the world's goods have no platform of their own.

The problem

A Swiggy rider, an Uber driver and an Amazon Flex courier are often the same person — but no platform connects those identities. More than half of gig workers operate across several platforms at once, and each one sees only its own slice.

Why it compounds

Without a single verifiable work history, banks read gig workers as unemployed and refuse them credit. The fragmentation is not an inconvenience; it is the thing keeping people out of the financial system.

What I do about it

I co-founded Gigzen in 2026 and build its software: the rider platform, the backend it runs on, the testing tool that proves it works, and the record of every test that was run — including the ones that failed.
