N° 04 — Live proof

Don’t take my word for it. Run it.

The check at the heart of kessler, running on your own device: a Monte Carlo estimate of the probability that two objects collide, computed on your graphics processing unit (GPU), compared with the exact answer.

Collision probability, two ways

Each sample draws a possible position of one object relative to the other from its uncertainty. The share of samples that land inside the combined hard-body radius is the probability of collision. The exact value comes from integrating the same distribution over that circle.

N° 10 — About

Correctness first, then speed.

I am a machine learning systems engineer from Bhubaneswar, Odisha. I work at the layer most people never see: the engines that serve language models, the loops that train them, the compilers and kernels under both, and the tests that prove they are right.

I hold my work to one standard: every number is reproducible from a script in the repository, including the cases where my system loses.
