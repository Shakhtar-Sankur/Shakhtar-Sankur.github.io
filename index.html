Government & public science · 12

  1. crash → 84/84

    European Space Agency · pagmo2 · the Advanced Concepts Team’s optimisation library

    Monotonic basin hopping crashed whenever its inner optimiser left the bounds, a bug open since May 2024. It now perturbs around the nearest point inside them.

    Dario Izzo · Co-author of pagmo2 · merged it the day I opened it. #660

  2. 1,808 tests pass

    ITER Organization ×2 · IMAS-Python · data library of the international fusion-energy project

    imas convert now takes a list of IDSs (Interface Data Structures) and occurrences and stops before writing if one is missing; a second fix pins the data-dictionary version the tests need.

    Olivier Hoenen · ITER · assigned me the issue, approved and merged both. Looks good to me now. #152 #151

  3. dy=3: 1 → 1.5

    US National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) · FiPy · finite-volume partial differential equation (PDE) solver

    With integer grid spacing, Grid2D truncated the distances from boundary faces to their cells. They are now computed in float64, as the reviewer asked.

    Jonathan Guyer · NIST · one of FiPy’s authors · merged it. #1243

  4. no build → 20/20

    US Department of Energy · Kokkos · GPU library of the national laboratories

    Kokkos::sort did not compile on NVIDIA CUDA or AMD HIP for types without a default constructor; it now sorts an index permutation. In the Kokkos 5.4 release notes.

    Damien Lebrun-Grandié · Leads Kokkos, Los Alamos · merged it after approval by Daniel Arndt, Oak Ridge. Generally the approach looks good. #9738

  5. wrong sign → fixed

    Idaho National Laboratory · MOOSE · Multiphysics Object-Oriented Simulation Environment, the US Department of Energy laboratory’s open-source simulation framework

    The Jacobian (the derivative Newton’s method uses) of the electromagnetics module’s transient absorbing boundary condition had the wrong sign and a mismatched coupling term. I fixed it and, at the module author’s request, made the admittance complex, with a deprecation so downstream apps keep working; two new Jacobian tests, 100% of new lines covered.

    Casey Icenhour · Wrote the electromagnetics module, Idaho National Laboratory · approved it; Guillaume Giudicelli approved and merged it. thanks for the contribution #34496

  6. crash → time coord

    UK Met Office · Iris · weather and climate data in Python

    iris.pandas.as_cubes crashed when a date-time column held one value in every row. It now becomes a time coordinate with the right units and calendar.

    Martin Yeo · UK Met Office · approved and merged it. Thanks @Shakhtar-Sankur! #7375

  7. 6,502 tests pass

    UK Met Office & Science and Technology Facilities Council (STFC) · PSyclone · code generator of the LFRic weather and climate model

    Fortran BIND(C) bindings were dropped and BIND(C) functions crashed it. Both fixed; entry 82 of its changelog.

    PSyclone maintainer · Ran the integration tests, approved and merged it. ITs passed #3668

  8. 32-bit → 64-bit

    Barcelona Supercomputing Center ×2 · Autosubmit · Spain’s climate-model workflow manager

    Job timestamps overflowed 32-bit PostgreSQL columns and crashed autosubmit run. Invited for the 4.2.0 release and credited in its changelog, then a second fix the maintainer asked for.

    Bruno P. Kinoshita · Autosubmit maintainer · merged both. Thanks (again) @Shakhtar-Sankur !!! #3405 #3406

  9. offline crash → skipped

    Estonian Information System Authority ×2 · libdigidocpp · Estonia’s digital-signature library

    Signing tests crashed without a network connection and are now skipped offline; the Online Certificate Status Protocol (OCSP) time-check notes now match the code.

    Raul Metsma · Maintainer · approved both. Merged by Kristel Merilain.. Thanks, both rules now match the code. #777 #775

Things I built

Each one checked against an independent reference.

I work from Bhubaneswar, India, mostly on one NVIDIA T4 graphics card in public Kaggle notebooks, so anyone can rerun the numbers below.

  • 53/53kessler

    NASA Conjunction Assessment Risk Analysis (CARA) Monte Carlo collision probability on all 53 test cases, 15–19× faster per core; screens 531 million object pairs in 28 s on one T4.

  • 62/71chirp

    An independent GPU search for gravitational waves that finds 62 of 71 LIGO O4a events blind.

  • 28.7×syndrome

    A GPU decoder for IBM’s quantum codes, bit-identical to IBM’s Relay-BP and 28.7× faster per shot.

  • 1.00× cuBLAStessel

    A tile language and compiler in Rust: matrix multiplication (GEMM) at parity with NVIDIA cuBLAS, attention 1.3–1.9× faster than PyTorch’s.

  • 0.75%samebit

    Qwen2.5-0.5B run entirely in integers, within 0.75% of float32, the same bits on a GPU and a CPU.

  • 23/24lipi

    A tokenizer that beats GPT-4o’s and Gemini’s in 23 of 24 Indian languages.

  • 5.2 → 0.04ratchet

    Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), a reinforcement-learning method for large language models, built from scratch; found 16-bit (fp16) weight sync silently erasing training updates.

  • 8.8 / 9.0larzavan

    Open global earthquake early warning: Tohoku’s magnitude from satellite-positioning (GNSS) data, held out of the fit.

  • 60 kills

    relay · switchyard · kiln · quorumdb

    Large language model (LLM) serving that survives 60 worker kills, mixture-of-experts 2.2× faster than Hugging Face, a machine learning compiler beating torch.compile, and a distributed SQL database.
